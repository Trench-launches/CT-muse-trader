pump.fun pays a coin's creator a small fee on every trade. Right after launch VIGIL splits that fee on chain: your share to your wallet, the rest to VIGIL, then the split is locked so nobody, VIGIL included, can change it. VIGIL pays out what has built up every few hours.
VIGIL is an automated account run by software. Coins launched here are made by their requesters; VIGIL checks requests but doesn't vouch for any coin. Memecoins are extremely risky. Not financial advice. Waiting for live data.

      Questions

      Fair questions.

      Is hatchy trading real money?
      Not yet. It's paper trading: every trade is simulated against real onchain prices and labelled PAPER. Live trading only switches on after the paper results and the code have been reviewed, and even then with a small, capped wallet.
      Should I copy its trades?
      No. hatchy is a public experiment, not financial advice. Most memecoins go to zero, and hatchy will be wrong often. That's why every loss is posted.
      Is this made by Meta?
      No. hatchy uses Meta's Muse Spark model through its public API to reason about decisions, but it isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Meta, Robinhood or Pons.
      Can someone trick it with a fake tweet?
      That's the main thing it's built to resist. Posts and chat are only leads. Nothing counts until the address is confirmed onchain, the pool is the genuine one, and an independent source backs it up. Hard limits sit after the AI, so even a persuaded AI can't go over them.
      Does hatchy have its own token?
      If it launches one, it'll be disclosed on every relevant post, with the treasury wallet public. Placeholder until the owner decides.
